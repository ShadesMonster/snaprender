How Brazilian Take-Home Pay Is Calculated

In Brazil, your take-home pay (salário líquido) is your gross salary minus two main deductions: INSS (social security contributions) and IRPF (income tax). Unlike many countries, Brazil calculates these deductions monthly, and INSS is deducted before IRPF is calculated, reducing your taxable base.

IRPF — Imposto de Renda Pessoa Física

IRPF is Brazil's progressive individual income tax, administered by the Receita Federal (federal revenue service). The 2024 monthly brackets are:

  • Up to R$2,259.20/month: exempt (0%)
  • R$2,259.21 to R$2,826.65/month: 7.5% (deduction R$169.44)
  • R$2,826.66 to R$3,751.05/month: 15% (deduction R$381.44)
  • R$3,751.06 to R$4,664.68/month: 22.5% (deduction R$662.77)
  • Above R$4,664.68/month: 27.5% (deduction R$896.00)

The "deduction" listed with each bracket is a simplified calculation shortcut that produces the same result as computing progressive rates across each bracket. Each dependent reduces your IRPF taxable base by R$189.59 per month.

INSS — Instituto Nacional do Seguro Social

INSS is Brazil's mandatory social security system that funds retirement pensions, disability benefits, and other social protections. Employee contributions in 2024 use progressive rates:

  • Up to R$1,412.00/month: 7.5%
  • R$1,412.01 to R$2,666.68/month: 9%
  • R$2,666.69 to R$4,000.03/month: 12%
  • R$4,000.04 to R$7,786.02/month: 14%

INSS has a contribution ceiling: the maximum monthly contribution is approximately R$908.85. Salaries above R$7,786.02/month do not incur additional INSS charges. Importantly, INSS is deducted from your gross salary before IRPF is calculated, so higher INSS contributions slightly reduce your income tax.

13º Salário — The Thirteenth Salary

Brazilian labor law (CLT — Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho) mandates that all formal employees receive a 13th salary payment, equal to one month's gross pay. This is typically paid in two installments: the first between February and November, and the second by December 20th. The 13º salário is subject to both INSS and IRPF, calculated separately from regular monthly pay.

CLT Employment in Brazil

CLT (Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho) is Brazil's comprehensive labor code governing formal employment. CLT workers receive various mandatory benefits beyond salary: 13º salário, paid vacation (30 days plus one-third bonus), FGTS deposits (8% of salary deposited by the employer), and transportation vouchers. This calculator focuses on the employee-side deductions — INSS and IRPF — that reduce your gross salary to your net take-home pay.

Tips to Optimize Your Take-Home Pay

  • Declare dependents: Each dependent reduces your monthly IRPF taxable base by R$189.59, potentially saving hundreds of reais per year.
  • Keep medical receipts: Medical and education expenses can be deducted in your annual IRPF declaration, potentially generating a tax refund.
  • Contribute to private pension (PGBL): PGBL contributions up to 12% of gross income can be deducted from taxable income in the annual declaration.
  • Understand your benefits: CLT benefits like FGTS (employer-paid), transportation vouchers, and meal vouchers add significant value beyond your cash salary.
