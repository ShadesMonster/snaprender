Why Calculate Meeting Costs?

Meetings are one of the most expensive activities in any organization. A one-hour meeting with 8 people isn't one hour of cost — it's eight hours of highly-paid professionals' time. Understanding the true cost helps teams make better decisions about when a meeting is necessary versus when an email or async update would do.

How We Calculate It

We convert each attendee's annual salary to an hourly rate (based on standard working hours for your country), multiply by the meeting duration, and sum across all attendees. The result is a conservative estimate — it doesn't include opportunity cost, context-switching time, or the cost of delayed work.
