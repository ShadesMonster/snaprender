How the Salary to Hourly Conversion Works

To convert an annual salary to an hourly rate, divide the salary by the total number of working hours in a year. The standard formula is:

Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × Working Weeks per Year)

Most full-time employees in the US work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, giving 2,080 work hours per year. Subtracting paid holidays and vacation days reduces the actual hours worked, which increases the effective hourly rate.

US Median Income Comparison

The median household income in the United States is approximately $74,580 per year (2023 Census data), which works out to about $35.86/hour for a standard 40-hour work week. The median individual income is approximately $40,480, or about $19.46/hour.
