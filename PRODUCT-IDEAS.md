# OneTab — Product Ideas & Research

> Research compiled Feb 2026. Based on SEO data, competitor analysis, viral tool case studies, and the freelancer/remote-worker ecosystem.

---

## The Opportunity

Calculator sites are **quietly massive**. A few proof points:

| Site | Niche | Monthly Organic Traffic | Pages |
|------|-------|------------------------|-------|
| OmniCalculator | Everything | **7M** | 3,700+ |
| InchCalculator | Construction/DIY | **4.5M** | 1,000s |
| Percentage Calculator | Math | **1.6M** | 3 pages total |
| TDEECalculator.net | Fitness (calories) | **1M** | 1 page |
| Calculator.net | Everything | **29M** | 1,000s |
| MortgageCalculator.org | Mortgages | **360K** (peaked 3M) | Dozens |
| FatCalc.com | Body fat | **150K** | 11 pages |
| SnowDayCalculator.com | Weather/school | **280K** (1M peak) | 1 page |
| AirFryerCalculator.com | Cooking | **18K** | 1 page, 3 inputs |

Key takeaways:
- **You don't need many pages.** Percentage Calculator gets 1.6M visits from 3 pages. FatCalc gets 150K from 11.
- **Single-purpose wins.** TDEECalculator = 1 page, 1M visits. AirFryerCalculator = 3 inputs, 18K visits.
- **Calculators survive algorithm updates.** OmniCalculator was untouched by Google's Helpful Content Update.
- **They attract backlinks naturally.** FatCalc has links from NY Times and The Telegraph with only 381 total backlinks.

Sources: [Ahrefs: 8 Websites Driving Insane Traffic Using Calculators](https://ahrefs.com/blog/website-calculators/), [CreativeWidgets: 10 Calculator Websites Dominating SEO](https://creativewidgets.io/blog/calculator-websites-seo)

## Strategy: Where OneTab Should Play

OneTab's positioning — **"Simple, useful tools. No signup, no nonsense."** — is perfect for this. The freelance rate calculator is a strong start. The next tools should:

1. **Target high-volume keywords** in the work/money/freelance space
2. **Be shareable** (results people screenshot or link to)
3. **Complement each other** (same audience = repeat visitors)
4. **Be simple** (one page, instant results, no friction)

---

## TIER 1: High-Traffic SEO Plays

These target keywords with massive search volume. They're competitive but winnable with good UX and the right long-tail angles.

### 1. Salary-to-Hourly / Hourly-to-Salary Converter
**Why:** "Salary to hourly calculator" is an enormous keyword. Every job listing triggers this search. Calculator.net and OmniCalculator rank for it, but the UX is mediocre.

**What to build:**
- Enter salary → get hourly rate (and vice versa)
- Factor in: hours/week, weeks/year, paid holidays
- Show weekly, biweekly, monthly, annual breakdowns
- Country presets (US, UK, EU, AU) for standard work hours
- Compare: "You make $X/hr — here's where that sits vs national median"

**Synergy with existing:** Direct complement to the freelance rate calculator. Same audience.

**Difficulty:** Medium. Lots of competition but room for better UX.

---

### 2. Take-Home Pay / Paycheck Calculator
**Why:** "Paycheck calculator" and "take home pay calculator" are some of the highest-traffic finance keywords. SmartAsset gets millions of visits from theirs.

**What to build:**
- Enter gross salary → see net pay after taxes
- Federal + state tax calculations (US focus first, expand to UK/AU/CA)
- Include: Social Security, Medicare, state tax, 401k deduction
- Show: effective tax rate, marginal rate, monthly/biweekly/weekly breakdown
- Optionally: "What if I move to [state]?" comparison

**Synergy:** Freelancers constantly need to understand net vs gross. Pairs with rate calculator.

**Programmatic SEO angle:** Create pages for every US state: "Texas Paycheck Calculator", "California Take Home Pay Calculator" — 50+ pages from one template.

**Difficulty:** Hard (tax logic is complex). High reward.

---

### 3. Compound Interest Calculator
**Why:** NerdWallet's compound interest calculator drives 33.7% of their /calculators/ subfolder traffic. This is a proven traffic magnet.

**What to build:**
- Initial investment, monthly contribution, interest rate, time period
- Visual chart showing growth over time (line graph)
- Show: total contributions vs total interest earned
- Comparison mode: "What if I invest $100/mo more?"
- Inflation-adjusted option

**Synergy:** Freelancers saving for retirement without employer 401k match. "You charge $X/hr — here's what saving 15% of that looks like in 20 years."

**Difficulty:** Easy to build. Medium competition.

---

### 4. Percentage Calculator
**Why:** 1.6M monthly visits to a 3-page site. People Google "what is X% of Y" constantly — during reporting, invoicing, discounts, tip calculations.

**What to build:**
- What is X% of Y?
- X is what percent of Y?
- Percentage increase/decrease from X to Y
- Clean, instant, zero-friction (answer visible on page load as you type)

**Synergy:** Useful for everyone. Gateway tool that brings traffic to the site → funnels to other tools.

**Difficulty:** Very easy to build. Hard to rank (competitive) but worth having.

## TIER 2: Shareable / Viral Tools

These are designed to be shared. The results are personal, interesting, or provocative enough that people screenshot them or send the link.

### 5. "What's My Meeting Actually Costing?" Calculator
**Why:** This is a *feelings* tool. Every office worker has sat in a pointless meeting. Viral potential is huge — people love sharing "this 1-hour meeting cost $4,700."

**What to build:**
- Number of attendees, average salary (or role presets), meeting duration
- Calculate: total cost of the meeting in dollars
- Show: "This meeting costs $78/minute" or "That's equivalent to [X]"
- Fun comparisons: "This meeting costs more than a round-trip flight to Miami"
- Share button: generate a screenshot-ready result card

**Viral mechanics:** LinkedIn gold. "I calculated the cost of our weekly all-hands. It's $312,000/year." Every middle manager will share this.

**Difficulty:** Very easy to build. High shareability.

---

### 6. "Am I Being Paid Fairly?" / Salary Comparison Tool
**Why:** Salary transparency is a massive cultural moment. People desperately want to know if they're underpaid. This is emotional, shareable, and high-intent.

**What to build:**
- Enter: role, experience, location, current salary
- Show: percentile ranking ("You earn more than 62% of [role] in [city]")
- Visualization: bell curve with "you are here" marker
- Suggest: "Based on your profile, the market range is $X–$Y"
- Source data from public datasets (BLS, Glassdoor ranges, Levels.fyi-style)

**Viral mechanics:** "I'm in the 38th percentile for my role..." → immediate sharing on Twitter/LinkedIn/Reddit.

**Difficulty:** Medium (need salary data — could start with BLS public datasets or curated ranges).

---

### 7. "What Should I Charge?" Quick Estimator
**Why:** Different angle than the existing rate calculator. This is faster and more opinionated — give me a number in 10 seconds.

**What to build:**
- 3 questions: What do you do? Where are you? How experienced are you?
- Instant answer: "You should charge at least $X/hr or $Y/project"
- Confidence meter: "85% of [role] in [city] charge between $X–$Y"
- One-click share: result card with your rate range
- CTA: "Want a deeper breakdown? Try our full rate calculator →"

**Viral mechanics:** Fast + opinionated = shareable. People will share their result and argue about it.

**Difficulty:** Easy. Uses similar data to existing calculator. Great funnel to the main tool.

---

### 8. Freelance Income Tax Estimator (Quarterly)
**Why:** Every freelancer in the US dreads quarterly estimated taxes. "How much should I set aside?" is a constant anxiety.

**What to build:**
- Enter: expected annual freelance income, filing status, state
- Show: estimated quarterly tax payment, effective rate, what to set aside per month
- Calendar: next quarterly deadline with countdown
- "Set aside this much from every invoice" calculation
- Expand to UK (self-assessment), Canada, Australia

**Viral mechanics:** Tax season = seasonal traffic spike. Shareable anxiety ("I need to set aside $X/quarter...").

**Difficulty:** Medium. Reuse tax logic from existing calculator.

## TIER 3: Freelancer Ecosystem (Builds Loyalty & Internal Traffic)

These serve the same audience as the rate calculator and create a "toolkit" effect where freelancers bookmark the whole site.

### 9. Invoice Generator (PDF)
**Why:** "Free invoice generator" is a massive keyword. Plutio, Zoho, Invoice Simple all compete here. OneTab already has a PDF rendering engine in the backend — this is a natural fit.

**What to build:**
- Clean invoice template with customizable fields
- Logo upload, client details, line items, tax, total
- Generate as PDF (use the existing SnapRender PDF API!)
- Save to localStorage for reuse
- No signup required (key differentiator vs most tools)

**Synergy:** Uses the existing backend infrastructure. Freelancer who calculates their rate → generates an invoice at that rate.

**Difficulty:** Medium. Already have the PDF engine. Huge keyword.

---

### 10. Project Cost Estimator
**Why:** The existing rate calculator has a "Project Cost" tab, but a standalone tool with more depth could rank for "project cost calculator" and "how to price a project."

**What to build:**
- Break project into phases/tasks
- Assign hours and hourly rate to each
- Add: materials, software, subcontractors, markup
- Show: total cost, suggested price with margin, per-milestone breakdown
- Export as PDF proposal (use SnapRender API)

**Difficulty:** Medium. Extends existing functionality.

---

### 11. Cost of Living Comparison Tool
**Why:** Remote workers choosing where to live. "Cost of living calculator" has massive search volume. NerdWallet, Numbeo, and Salary.com are the big players but are bloated with ads.

**What to build:**
- Compare two cities side by side
- Categories: housing, food, transport, healthcare, taxes
- "Your $X salary in [City A] = $Y in [City B]"
- Focus on remote worker angle: "Best cities for remote workers earning $X"
- Use public data (BLS, Numbeo API, or curated dataset)

**Difficulty:** Hard (data sourcing). High traffic potential.

---

### 12. Time Zone Meeting Planner
**Why:** Every remote team deals with this. "What time is it in [city]" is one of the most Googled queries on Earth.

**What to build:**
- Add 2-5 cities
- Visual timeline showing overlap of working hours
- Highlight "golden hours" where everyone is awake and working
- Click to copy: "Let's meet at 3pm EST / 8pm GMT / 7am AEST"
- Save favorite city combos to localStorage

**Difficulty:** Easy-medium. Lots of competition but most tools are ugly or bloated.

## TIER 4: Programmatic SEO Opportunities

Build one template, generate dozens or hundreds of pages, capture long-tail search traffic.

### 13. "[State] Paycheck Calculator" (50 pages)
Template: one paycheck calculator, parameterized by state tax rules.
- "California paycheck calculator" / "Texas take home pay" / etc.
- Each page is SEO-optimized for that state
- SmartAsset does this and gets millions of visits

### 14. "[Role] Salary Guide" (20-50 pages)
Template: one salary comparison page, parameterized by job role.
- "Web developer salary 2026" / "Graphic designer salary guide" / etc.
- Show: average, median, range, by city, by experience level
- Link to: rate calculator ("convert this salary to an hourly rate →")

### 15. "[City A] vs [City B] Cost of Living" (100+ pages)
Template: one comparison page, parameterized by city pairs.
- "San Francisco vs Austin cost of living" / "NYC vs London" / etc.
- Pre-generate the most popular comparisons
- Each is a unique landing page for search

### 16. "Freelance [Role] Rates in [Country]" (50+ pages)
Template: one rate guide page, parameterized by role + country.
- "Freelance web developer rates UK" / "Freelance designer rates Australia"
- Pulls from existing rate calculator data
- Links to the full calculator for personalized results

## TIER 5: Fun / Wildcard (High Virality, Lower SEO)

### 17. "Can I Afford To Quit?" Calculator
- Enter: savings, monthly expenses, freelance pipeline
- Shows: "You have X months of runway"
- Share-worthy for people considering the leap to freelance

### 18. "Raise Calculator" — What's a 5% Raise Actually Worth?
- Enter current salary and raise percentage
- Show: actual dollar increase, after-tax increase, per-paycheck increase
- "Your 3% raise is $47/paycheck after taxes" — shareable reality check

### 19. "Side Hustle Revenue Calculator"
- Hours per week on side project, hourly equivalent, months
- "If you spend 10hrs/week on this for 6 months at $50/hr equivalent, that's $13,000"
- Motivational angle — designed to be screenshotted

### 20. "How Much Time Are You Spending in Meetings?" Audit
- Enter: number of recurring meetings, duration, frequency
- Annual total: "You spend 1,240 hours/year in meetings — that's 31 work weeks"
- Viral outrage potential

## Recommended Build Order

Priority based on: **effort vs. impact**, synergy with existing tools, and SEO potential.

### Phase 1 — Quick Wins (1-2 days each)
1. **Salary ↔ Hourly Converter** — Easy build, huge keyword, direct complement
2. **Meeting Cost Calculator** — Very easy, high viral potential
3. **Percentage Calculator** — Dead simple, massive search volume

### Phase 2 — Core Toolkit (3-5 days each)
4. **Compound Interest Calculator** — Proven traffic magnet, moderate effort

6. **Quarterly Tax Estimator** — Reuses existing tax logic, seasonal traffic

### Phase 3 — Traffic Machines (1-2 weeks each)
7. **Take-Home Pay Calculator** — Complex but enormous traffic potential

9. **Cost of Living Comparison** — Hard (data) but massive keyword

### Phase 4 — Viral Plays (1-3 days each)
10. **"What Should I Charge?" Quick Estimator** — Fast, opinionated, shareable
11. **"Can I Afford To Quit?"** — Emotional, shareable
12. **Raise Calculator** — Quick, reality-check angle

## Key Design Principles (From Research)

1. **Keep it dead simple.** AirFryerCalculator = 3 inputs, generates traffic. Don't over-engineer.
2. **Design for screenshots, not share buttons.** The Viral Post Generator went viral because people screenshotted results — no share API needed.
3. **Results should feel personal.** "You are here" on a bell curve > raw numbers.
4. **Seasonal traffic is real.** Tax calculators spike in Jan–April. Snow day calculators spike in winter. Build for these cycles.
5. **Programmatic SEO scales.** One template + 50 variations = 50 ranking opportunities.
6. **Internal linking is the moat.** Every tool should link to 2-3 others. "Calculated your hourly rate? Now see your take-home pay →"
